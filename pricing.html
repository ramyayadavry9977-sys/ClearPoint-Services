FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Our Pricing?

Here are some common questions about our window cleaning services and pricing.

How is the final price determined?

Pricing can depend on the size of your property, number of windows, accessibility, window type, and the service you choose. Contact us for a more accurate quote.

Do you offer recurring cleaning plans?

Yes. We can arrange recurring cleaning visits based on your preferred schedule, including regular residential and commercial service.

Are interior and exterior windows included?

This depends on the service plan you choose. Our Complete Shine and Commercial options can include both interior and exterior cleaning.

Do you clean commercial buildings?

Yes. We provide window cleaning solutions for commercial properties and can create a service plan based on the property's requirements.

Can I request a custom quote?

Absolutely. If your property needs a custom service or has special requirements, contact our team and we can discuss the available options.
