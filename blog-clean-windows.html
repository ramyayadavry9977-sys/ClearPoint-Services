WINDOW CLEANING TIPS

7 Simple Ways to Keep Your Windows Clean

Learn simple and effective ways to keep your windows looking clear, fresh, and well-maintained.

ClearPoint Services

Why Clean Windows Matter

Clean windows can make a noticeable difference to the look and feel of your home or business. Regular cleaning removes dust, dirt, fingerprints, and other buildup while helping your windows stay clear and well-maintained.

The good news is that keeping your windows clean does not always require complicated equipment. With a few simple habits and the right cleaning techniques, you can maintain a clearer view throughout the year.

01

Dust Before You Clean

Start by removing loose dust and dirt from the glass, frames, and window tracks. This prevents dirt from spreading across the glass when you begin wiping it.

02

Use the Right Cleaning Solution

Choose a suitable glass-cleaning solution and avoid using excessive amounts. The right solution helps remove fingerprints and marks without leaving unnecessary residue behind.

03

Clean From Top to Bottom

Work from the top of the window toward the bottom. This helps prevent dirty water from running over areas that you have already cleaned.

04

Use a Microfiber Cloth

A clean microfiber cloth is useful for wiping glass and removing remaining moisture. It can help reduce streaks and leave the surface looking cleaner.

05

Do Not Forget the Window Frames

Glass is only one part of a clean window. Wipe the frames, edges, and tracks regularly to remove accumulated dust and dirt.

06

Choose the Right Time to Clean

Cleaning during extremely hot or sunny conditions can cause cleaning solution to dry quickly. Choosing a suitable time can make the cleaning process easier.

07

Consider Professional Window Cleaning

For large homes, difficult-to-reach windows, or commercial properties, professional window cleaning can make regular maintenance more convenient.

CLEANING GUIDE

Common Window Cleaning Mistakes

Avoid these common mistakes to make your window cleaning routine more effective.

01

Using Too Much Cleaning Solution

Using too much solution can leave residue on the glass and make it harder to achieve a clear finish.

02

Using Rough Materials

Rough or unsuitable materials can leave marks or scratches. Use appropriate soft cleaning materials instead.

03

Ignoring Window Frames

Cleaning only the glass leaves dust and dirt around the frames and tracks. Remember to clean the complete window area.

04

Cleaning in Direct Sunlight

Strong sunlight can cause cleaning solution to dry quickly, which may make streaks more noticeable.
